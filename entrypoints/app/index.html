Export the words you look up most:

Install language data

Installs the lemma dictionary and frequency list for the selected language (built from PoliMorf / lemmatization-lists / OpenSubtitles). Polish, German and English ship with the extension; other languages are downloaded. Without it, word forms are tracked literally and inflections of the same word count separately.

Install from local files instead

Pick the two TSV artifacts built by scripts/build-lang-data.mjs.

Backup & restore

One JSON file with everything: your words in every language, the whole library, and your settings — for moving to a new PC or surviving a reset. (Dictionaries and frequency lists aren't included; language setup re-downloads them.)

Import vocabulary

Auto-detects the format: the Lute database (lute.db, read directly), a Lute term CSV export, an Anki "Notes in Plain Text" export, or the CSV from manga-translator / language-reactor-clone. Statuses are mapped: 1–5 → learning stages, W / Well Known → known, I → ignored. Words are lemmatized on import; existing entries are never downgraded; multi-word terms are skipped.

Estimate your vocabulary

You'll see words sampled across the frequency spectrum — answer honestly whether you know each one. The quiz adapts as it goes, so it needs about 20 questions instead of 38.

Some of the words are fake. They look Polish but don't exist. That's not a trick — it measures how often you'd claim a word you don't actually know, and corrects your score for it. Nobody scores zero on those, so don't worry about it.

Requires installed language data (Languages tab).

What this will change

Words you almost certainly know are marked known. The uncertain band is marked learning rather than claimed outright — that's the honest reading of the estimate. Anything below is left alone. Words you've already tracked are never overwritten.
